# BryantChang 博客头像

使用内置 image_gen 生成，用于博客侧栏的圆形头像。视觉包含 BC 字母、BryantChang 字标和互联的存储节点，强调个人标识与分布式存储。

站点资源：`assets/img/avatar-bryantchang-storage.png`。通过 `_config.yml` 的 `avatar` 和 `avatar_alt` 配置引用，保留主题的圆形裁切和首页链接。

## 生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final square avatar for BryantChang's technical blog, displayed as a 112-pixel circle.
Primary request: create a distinctive personal emblem combining BryantChang's identity with distributed storage.
Subject and composition: one bold, beautifully constructed interlocking "BC" monogram at the center; integrate three compact stacked storage-node symbols and a few crisp connecting paths around the monogram, communicating replicated data across a small distributed cluster. A subtle circular topology brings the mark together. Include the exact wordmark "BryantChang" beneath the large monogram, inside the circle, in clean, strong lettering. The BC is the dominant shape.
Style: refined contemporary technology identity, premium editorial design, precise geometry, restrained dimensional depth, confident and approachable. Strong silhouette, very few components, no technical-diagram labels.
Palette: deep ink blue background with luminous ivory lettering and restrained blue and teal highlights, compatible with a light gray and blue technical blog.
Framing: 1:1 square canvas, centered emblem. All lettering and important nodes stay comfortably inside a circle occupying 80% of the image width, safe for circular cropping. Solid background extends to every corner. The monogram must remain immediately legible when scaled to a tiny avatar.
Text verbatim: "BC" and "BryantChang" only. Spell BryantChang exactly B-r-y-a-n-t-C-h-a-n-g, with capital B and C.
Avoid: people, faces, mascots, generic brain icons, cloud icons, brand logos, dense circuit-board details, tiny annotations, interface mockups, photo backgrounds, watermark, excessive bloom. Render one finished avatar, not a presentation sheet or multiple variants.
```
